We are responsible for the personal details you give us through this website.

What we collect and why

  • Quote and contact requests. When you use the quote form, email, phone or WhatsApp us, we receive your name, contact details and what you tell us about the job. We use this only to reply, visit, quote and carry out the work. The quote form is delivered to our inbox by FormSubmit (formsubmit.co).
  • Customers. If you hire us, we keep your details and invoices for as long as Spanish tax law requires (normally 6 years).
  • Enquiries that don't go ahead are deleted within 12 months.

The legal basis is taking steps at your request before a contract, the contract itself, and our legal obligations. For advertising cookies it is your consent, which you can withdraw at any time.

Cookies

If you click Accept on the cookie banner, we use advertising cookies from Google and Meta. They let us show our ads to people who have already visited this website (remarketing) and see how many enquiries come from those ads. If you click Reject, or don't choose, none of these are set and nothing is sent to them.

Who else sees your data

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your details, object to or limit how we use them, or take a copy with you. Email info@handymanaxarquia.com. If you're not happy with our answer you can complain to the Spanish data protection authority, the AEPD (www.aepd.es).
